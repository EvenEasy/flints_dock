# Flint’s Dock branding

The Apache-2.0 license for the code does not grant permission to present a
third-party product as Flint’s Dock or to use the project’s name, logos or mascot
as that product’s identity. See Section 6 of [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Third-party forks and redistributed products must use their own product name
and branding. Replace Flint’s Dock logos, mascot and branded artwork unless you
have permission from the relevant rights holders to use them for that purpose.
The separately identified artwork is covered by [ASSETS_LICENSE.md](ASSETS_LICENSE.md).

Do not imply that a fork, service or product is official, affiliated with,
partnered with, sponsored by or endorsed by the Flint’s Dock team without
permission. This applies to product names, logos, websites, app listings and
marketing materials.

You may truthfully describe where the code came from, retain copyright and
attribution notices, and link to the [original project](https://github.com/EvenEasy/flints_dock).
For example: “Based on the Flint’s Dock open-source code; an independently
maintained project.” Such references must not suggest official status or
endorsement.

This branding policy does not change Apache-2.0 or prohibit commercial use,
modification or competing forks of the code. Apache-2.0 attribution obligations
do not require a Flint’s Dock logo or a visible notice on every application
screen. Third-party names and marks remain subject to their own rights.

For a branding permission request, contact the project maintainers through the
original repository. Permission must come from the appropriate rights holders;
a maintainer’s role alone does not establish ownership of every asset.
